# User guide / उपयोगकर्ता मार्गदर्शिका

This is a simulated coordination prototype, not official yatra registration. Use only invented participant information during demonstrations.

## Pilgrim / यात्री

1. Choose Manimahesh and register a group. Supply leader/contact details, group size, member age bands, planned departure/return and consent. Other routes remain planned and unavailable.
2. Save or print the pass. The QR contains an opaque pass identifier, not contact details. Protect the pass like a private lookup code.
3. Open offline readiness while connected. Download and verify the resources. Resolve displayed blockers before testing airplane mode; simply opening the home page is insufficient.
4. Scan a proposed checkpoint QR or paste its token. Choose outbound, returning or final Hadsar exit; select precisely the members present. For a split group, record each subgroup separately.
5. “Locally saved / upload pending” means this phone only. Reconnect, keep the app open and sync. Confirm server receipt. Invalid events need correction; do not repeatedly create replacement records without checking the queue.
6. Self-reported exit remains pending staff confirmation. Reaching Manimahesh Lake does not complete the journey.
7. Use health concerns/SOS for the demonstration. Pending SOS has not reached a remote person. Server received does not mean dispatched. Acknowledgment is a separate staff action.

**हिन्दी:** पहले पास और ऑफलाइन संसाधन डाउनलोड करके जाँचें। बिना नेटवर्क रिकॉर्ड केवल इसी फोन पर रहता है। वापसी में उपस्थित सदस्यों को चुनें; हड़सर पर कर्मचारी की पुष्टि के बाद ही निकास पूरा होता है। झील पहुँचना यात्रा समाप्त होना नहीं है। एसओएस प्राप्त होना बचाव दल रवाना होने की पुष्टि नहीं है।

## Volunteer / स्वयंसेवक

Sign in using the locally generated volunteer account. Register a person without their own phone through the same registration form on the volunteer device; retain/print their pass with consent. In the volunteer screen scan the group pass or enter its short code, load its member roster while online, select checkpoint/token and members physically present, then record the observed count and direction. Format checking offline is not signature verification. Reconnect with your staff session to upload confirmed observations. Never mark absent members returned.

**हिन्दी:** केवल उपस्थित सदस्यों का चयन करें। समूह के एक व्यक्ति के लौटने पर पूरे समूह का निकास दर्ज न करें। बिना नेटवर्क स्वयंसेवक की प्रविष्टि भी स्थानीय रहती है; अपलोड के समय कर्मचारी सत्र आवश्यक हो सकता है।

## Control room / नियंत्रण कक्ष

Use an authorised role to inspect stored groups, flags, visit/receipt timestamps, sources and member states. Open the group timeline before assigning an incident. Record the reason, owner and notes; acknowledge received SOS/flags explicitly. Publish observations separately from operational advisories and externally sourced official bulletins; set scope and expiry. A source URL alone does not verify authenticity. Export only for an authorised purpose and remove the downloaded copy under the retention policy.

Read “overdue report” as a request for human follow-up, possibly caused by network delay. Check evidence and contact staff before drawing conclusions. The dashboard is not a live moving GPS map.

## Family / परिवार

Share only after explicit consent. Send the unguessable family URL privately; it expires and can be revoked. It shows a last checkpoint/time and journey state, not continuous location, contacts or staff notes. An old timestamp can mean delayed upload. If revoked/expired, generate a new consented link rather than sharing the full pilgrim pass.

## Route and weather

The real route view is indicative. It is not turn-by-turn navigation. GPX/GeoJSON imports must include provenance and offline rights; import does not grant route approval. Foreground GPS requires device permission and adequate accuracy; a controlled test track is clearly separated from the mountain route. Do not use this prototype to invent shortcuts.

Forecast retrieval and validity times matter. Cached forecasts can be stale; the endpoint does not supply model issue time. Staff observations, forecasts and official advisories are different evidence types. Consult actual local authorities for current permissions and conditions.
