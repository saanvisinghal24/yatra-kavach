# Yatra Kavach - Project Idea

## High-Altitude Himalayan Pilgrimages: The Safety Crisis

Every summer, tens of thousands of pilgrims embark on high-altitude yatras in Himachal Pradesh:
- **Manimahesh Yatra** (Chamba district): 13+ km steep ascent to 4,080 meters (Gaurikund / Manimahesh Lake).
- **Kinner Kailash Yatra** (Kinnaur district): Grueling trek reaching 4,650 meters facing extreme temperature drops.
- **Shrikhand Mahadev Yatra** (Kullu district): One of the toughest pilgrimages in India, ascending to 5,160 meters through glacier fields, boulder tracks, and thin air.

### The Realities on the Ground
1. **Severe Cellular Dead Zones:** Cellular reception drops within the first 2-3 km from trailheads. For the remaining 10-30 km, pilgrims have zero connectivity.
2. **Acute Mountain Sickness (AMS), HAPE, and HACE:** Pilgrims ascend thousands of vertical meters in single day pushes without proper acclimatization. Early AMS warning signs go unmonitored until life-threatening collapse occurs.
3. **Flash Floods, Cloudbursts, and Rapid Freezing:** Mountain weather changes within minutes. Checkpoints with no network cannot warn ascending groups to halt before exposed ridges.
4. **Disorganized Search and Rescue:** When a pilgrim goes missing, authorities do not know which 5 km mountain ravine they are in. Rescue teams are mobilized blindly across the entire mountain range.

---

## The Yatra Kavach Solution

**Yatra Kavach** (यंत्र कवच / सुरक्षा कवच) is an offline-first pilgrim safety and trail telemetry system designed specifically for the extreme conditions of Himachal Pradesh.

### Core Philosophy
1. **Physical Anchors, Offline Digital Scans:** Signed, weatherproof QR checkpoint boards placed every 1.5–3 km along the trail.
2. **Offline-First PWA:** Pilgrims and volunteer staff scan checkpoints directly in airplane mode. Events queue locally and automatically synchronize whenever intermittent network is detected or at base camp WiFi hubs.
3. **Deterministic Rules Engine:** A mathematically rigorous rule engine flags fast ascent, overdue travelers, missing groups, head-count drops, and missed cutoffs in real time.
4. **Control Room Telemetry:** Real-time trail density mapping, altitude progress ribbons, and early incident escalation for the District Administration, ITBP, and SDRF.
5. **Dignified, Human-Centric Design:** Built like an authentic survey trail board—calm, readable in direct mountain sunlight, bilingual (English & Hindi), and respectful of pilgrim privacy.
