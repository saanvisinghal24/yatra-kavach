# Three-minute demo

1. Open the landing page and state: simulated data, indicative trail values.
2. Register a group; save its pass and family link.
3. Visit scan once online, then disable networking. Scan two signed posters.
4. Show pending events at /demo/offline-check. Restore networking and sync.
5. Sign in to /control; inspect group history and alerts.
6. Load a missing-group scenario, inspect the R8 search window, assign an incident.
7. Show pilgrim status, family checkpoint and the SOS call/SMS options. No emergency service is automatically dispatched.

A signed static QR authenticates a checkpoint identifier, not physical presence: a photographed QR can be replayed at a different location.
