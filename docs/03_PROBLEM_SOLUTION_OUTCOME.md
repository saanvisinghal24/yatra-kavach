# Yatra Kavach - Problem, Solution, Outcome

## The Problem
Every year, high-altitude pilgrimage routes across Himachal Pradesh witness preventable medical crises, severe hypothermia, stampedes at bottleneck points, and agonizing searches for missing pilgrims.
- 0% cellular connectivity over 20+ km rugged stretches.
- Blind search-and-rescue operations over hundreds of square kilometers.
- Lack of continuous head-count reconciliation leading to stragglers lost in sub-zero nights.
- Information lag between mountain ridges and district disaster management rooms.

## The Solution: Yatra Kavach
A lightweight, offline-first digital safety umbrella:
- **Offline Checkpoints:** Physical signed QR markers turn any phone into a reliable tracking sensor without GPS or active internet.
- **Explainable Rules Engine:** Real-time flagging of speed anomalies, overdue segments, head-count drops, and weather risks before tragedies unfold.
- **Control Room Operations:** Instant bird's-eye visibility for district authorities and mountain police, locating distress down to precise 1.5 km intervals.
- **Bilingual & Empathetic:** High contrast, battery-efficient, accessible in Hindi and English, with direct SMS fallback for emergency services (112 / 108).

## The Outcome
- **Search radius reduced from 20 km to a 2 km segment.**
- **Zero data loss** in complete cellular blackouts.
- **Proactive AMS interventions** before acute pulmonary or cerebral edema occurs.
- Reassurance for families via secure, privacy-preserving, read-only status links.
